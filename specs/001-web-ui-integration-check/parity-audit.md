## Nav-model composition finding (fixed in this change, not a gap)

`src/app/(app)/_components/nav-model.ts` still carried a `workflows` nav entry (`{ key: "workflows", label: "Workflows", href: "/workflows" }`) pointing at a route that will never exist under that name (per PDR-017 above — `010-skill-chain-views-ui.md`'s own acceptance criteria confirm "no separate workflows navigation entry" is correct going forward). This is dead shell chrome left over from before the retirement decision, not a "not yet built" gap — removed as composition wiring (FR-002/FR-015). The `/metrics` and `/teams/{id}/policies` nav entries were deliberately **left in place**, since those point at features that are still planned (open backlog items above), not retired ones — removing them would misrepresent "not yet built" as "will never exist."

## Summary against Success Criteria

- **SC-001 / SC-003**: Every legacy route family has a documented classification above. 7 of 9 families are fully rebuilt/replaced with parity; 2 have a real, already-tracked ownership gap (governance views UI, skill-chain views UI) plus one already-open epic item for org-wide metrics.
- **SC-002**: No standalone shell, duplicate sidebar, duplicate account footer, or temporary auth/middleware stand-in was found anywhere in `(app)` — every page renders under the single `(app)/layout.tsx` gate (`resolveAppShellAccess`); `settings/audit-log/access.ts`'s `canAccessAuditLog` is a role-permission check layered *inside* the already-authenticated shell, not a competing auth gate.
